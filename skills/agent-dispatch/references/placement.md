# Placement Decision

Make the Placement Decision only after the Engagement Contract is ready. The decision belongs to the Lead and is not an automatic model router.

## Identify the work

Define the smallest independently verifiable Delegation Unit and assign one Work Shape:

- `Judgment`: form an interpretation, compare alternatives, or recommend a decision.
- `Execution`: realize fixed intent while retaining bounded local semantic judgment.
- `Mechanical`: apply a complete enumerable rule; return instead of inventing exceptions.

## Compare total completion cost

Assess Handoff Cost through concrete Routing Factors:

- context the Child would have to reconstruct;
- queueing and model latency;
- coordination needed to explain scope and authority;
- verification the Lead must repeat before acceptance;
- capability or isolation benefit gained from a Child.

Prefer Lead retention when the Lead already holds expensive context, the edit is narrow, or delegation would merely replay the Lead's reasoning before requiring the same verification. Prefer Dispatch only when the unit is Handoff-Ready and the expected Child benefit exceeds reconstruction, latency, coordination, and verification cost.

Record the decisive factors and accepted trade-off in prose. Do not manufacture numeric precision or optimize model price in isolation.

## Direct retention

After retaining, perform the work under the Engagement Contract. Record the final work state as Lead-sourced, validate it, and independently evaluate the Engagement Contract before delivery.

The current tracer bullet stops with `dispatch_candidate_not_executed` when Dispatch would win. Do not claim Handoff-Ready or spawn a Child until the Delegation Contract workflow is installed.
